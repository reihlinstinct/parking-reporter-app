This application uses a disclosed technical derivative: Jerusalem city-code3000 subset, EPSG2039 converted to WGS84, demographic fields removed, ring orientation normalized, then partitioned into versioned geographic files. Boundaries are not simplified or repaired. The2022 snapshot contains243statistical areas, not named neighborhoods. It does not establish current municipal boundaries. Internal non-Jerusalem enclaves remain holes. CBS does not sponsor or endorse this application.
